About Bharath

I bridge human needs, business goals and AI-powered products. From Hyderabad to Atlanta, one obsession has stayed the same: make complex things feel simple, and prove it with numbers.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer who also studies the systems behind the screen. After an MBA in Marketing & Digital Strategy and an M.S. in Business Analytics, I’m now pursuing a PhD in Information Technology & Infrastructure Management. My design work is backed by photography, video editing and drone cinematography.
